# The Distributed Mind Between Social Networks and Malla Services - N. Pecorari

## Overview
An essay exploring the extended mind thesis and its implications in the age of 
social media and large language models. The paper examines how digital 
technologies reshape human cognition — from attention and digital wellbeing to 
the erosion of cognitive skills — and closes with an analysis of Malla Services, 
malicious LLM-based tools used for cybercrime.

## Structure
- **The "new extension"** — the philosophical background of the extended mind 
  thesis (Clark & Chalmers), from Cartesian dualism to the "parity" and 
  "complementarity" principles
- **Overuse** — Digital Overuse (Fasoli) and digital akrasia: why digital 
  technologies challenge self-control, and the design mechanisms (infinite 
  newsfeeds, randomized rewards) that drive compulsive use
- **Deskilling** — the cognitive costs of technological "outsourcing," from GPS 
  navigation to LLMs, including their impact on writing and programming skills
- **Malla Services** — an analysis of malicious LLM-integrated services sold on 
  underground markets to generate malicious code, phishing content, and scam 
  websites
